Our games

Five games, all free.

Each one plays offline, keeps your progress on your phone and asks for no Android permissions.

Forkline

Tower defense

A cozy storybook village at dusk. Solve each map like a puzzle in the build phase, then watch your defense work as the waves come down the roads.

  • Gates you place to steer creatures down each fork
  • A campaign of 48 levels, each with stars and a Twist
  • Roguelite runs with card picks, and a daily challenge
  • A landscape layout on a foldable's inner screen

SumUp

Number puzzle

A calm, clever number puzzle. Drag across matching tiles to join them into one bigger number, then keep going and see how high you can climb.

  • Chains in eight directions, each tile the same or double
  • Boosters paid for with your score, never with money
  • Three complete looks: Gumdrop, Slate and Nova
Game site Source code Privacy

Updraft

Tilt to fly

A kid lets go of a little red balloon in the bedroom, and you take it from there. Tilt your phone to ride warm air out of the house, over the valley and beyond.

  • A red balloon or a folded paper plane
  • Sunbeams, kettle steam, thermals and ridge wind
  • An Endless country to explore, and a daily flight
Game site Source code Privacy

WoodLogic

Screw puzzle

A wooden picture hangs on the board, held on by colored screws. Tap a screw to spin it out and fill the boxes. Plan ahead, because the tray is small.

  • Levels made on your phone and checked by a solver
  • A Daily puzzle, and a Relaxed mode with no clock
  • Woods and metals to unlock just by playing
Game site Source code Privacy

Sudoku

Logic puzzle

Sudoku where every empty cell shows all nine candidates around its edge, and you cross them out by hand as you rule them out.

  • Five levels, from Beginner to Expert
  • Hints that point the way and never fill in for you
  • Lessons that teach each technique, and a dark look

Our promise

Every game follows the same rules.

No exceptions, and no fine print.

Free, for good

No ads and nothing to buy. Everything in the game is earned by playing.

No tracking

No accounts, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Fully offline

The games never connect to the internet and ask for no Android permissions.

Your phone, your progress

Saves stay on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Open source

Every game's source code is public, under the GNU GPL v3 with additional terms.

Made in code

Every picture, effect, sound and song is made by the game's own code.
