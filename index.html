Play, Inc.

Small, free games for Android phones, made by Bart Basi.

Every Play, Inc. game follows the same rules:

  • Free, with no ads and nothing to buy.
  • No accounts, no tracking and no analytics.
  • Works offline. The games never connect to the internet and ask for no Android permissions.
  • Your progress stays on your phone.
  • Open source, under the GNU GPL v3 with additional terms.
